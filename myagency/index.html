MyAgency™

Reclaim ownership of your attention, decisions, and direction.

A MyDopa™ framework for staying connected to your own path in a world of infinite information, ideas, opportunities, and possible futures.

We live in a world of almost unlimited possibility. There are more ideas to pursue, more people to learn from, more tools to use, more businesses to build, more content to consume, and more possible versions of your future than any previous generation has experienced.

That abundance can be exhilarating. It can also make it surprisingly difficult to know what deserves your attention, stay with one direction, and feel that you are actually moving forward.

MyAgency™ is MyDopa™'s framework for understanding and strengthening your ability to own your attention, decisions, direction, and progress in a world constantly competing for them.

The problem is not necessarily a lack of ambition, ideas, or opportunity. Increasingly, the problem is maintaining your own direction while new possibilities keep appearing.

The Infinite Possibility Problem

What is happening to our sense of direction?

For much of history, opportunity was constrained by access. Today, the opposite problem is becoming common: an individual can encounter hundreds of possible careers, businesses, tools, creators, strategies, opinions, and lifestyles before breakfast.

The challenge is no longer simply discovering possibility. It is deciding which possibilities are yours — and staying with them long enough to discover what they can become.

Why can ambition start to feel like anxiety?

You can be highly ambitious and still feel scattered. You can continually learn while rarely feeling finished. You can have more ideas than ever while feeling less certain about which idea deserves your attention.

A familiar loop can emerge: discover something exciting, begin, encounter another possibility, question the original direction, pivot, and begin again.

Over time, you may accumulate enormous amounts of information without accumulating enough evidence that you can trust yourself to stay with something.

What does agency mean?

Agency is the sense that your choices and actions matter — that you can choose a direction, act on it, and influence what happens next.

Psychologist Martin Seligman's work on agency emphasizes the importance of efficacy, optimism, and imagination. Modern technology has dramatically expanded imagination: we can see more possible futures than ever before. But seeing more possibilities does not automatically make it easier to choose among them.

What is the infinite possibility problem?

Imagine having ten genuinely interesting ideas. AI makes each one look easier to build. Social media shows you people succeeding with completely different ideas. A podcast introduces another opportunity. A founder interview creates another. A new technology creates another.

Soon the problem is no longer finding something worth pursuing. The problem is staying oriented while everything around you keeps presenting another possible future.

Why doesn't more information automatically create more agency?

Information expands what you can see. It does not automatically tell you what deserves your life.

Research on information overload and choice overload has repeatedly examined what happens when the amount of information or number of options becomes difficult to evaluate. More possibility can increase cognitive demand instead of increasing clarity.

This creates a modern contradiction: we have extraordinary access to information about how to improve our lives, yet it can become harder to decide what deserves sustained attention.

From Possibility to Evidence

What changes when progress becomes visible?

When your attention is dominated by possibility, your reference point becomes everything you could be doing.

Visible progress changes the reference point from hypothetical possibility to lived reality.

Instead of only asking, "What else could I be doing?" you can ask: What have I actually done? What did I follow through on? What changed because I acted? What evidence do I have that I am moving?

Why might evidence strengthen agency?

One component of agency is efficacy: the belief that you can act effectively.

Evidence from your own life gives that belief something concrete to rest on. A completed action, a difficult conversation handled differently, a decision followed through on, a return after a setback, or a meaningful experience preserved can become evidence that your actions matter.

This is central to the MyDopa™ worldview: Confidence comes from evidence.

Can yesterday help anchor tomorrow?

There is an interesting real-world example in entrepreneur and coach Dan Sullivan's public discussion of his daily "great yesterday" practice.

Sullivan has described a tendency to become absorbed by future possibilities and has publicly associated the practice with feeling calmer, more present, and less mentally scattered. His experience is one person's account, not proof of a medical effect. But it raises an important question: what happens when attention is deliberately returned from imagined futures to evidence of what actually happened?

That question sits directly inside the MyAgency research territory.

What is the MyAgency hypothesis?

Visible progress may help people reclaim agency by strengthening their ability to trust their own actions, maintain direction, and remain connected to what they are actually building.

This is a research thesis, not a finished scientific conclusion. MyDopa™ intends to keep studying the relationship between visible progress, self-efficacy, attention, direction, and personal agency.

MyAgency and MyDopa™

What is the relationship between MyAgency and MyDopa™?

MyAgency™ names the larger human challenge.

MyDopa™ is being built to make personal progress visible.

When progress disappears from view, attention can easily be captured by the next possibility. When your actions become visible across time, you gain another reference point: your own evidence.

Do I need fewer ideas?

No. The goal is not to become less ambitious, stop learning, reject technology, or eliminate new ideas.

The goal is to become better at deciding which ideas deserve you.

Agency means being able to encounter possibility without automatically surrendering your direction to it.

What is MyAgency?

MyAgency™ is MyDopa™'s framework for reclaiming ownership of your attention, decisions, direction, and progress in a world constantly competing for them.

What is personal agency?

Personal agency is the sense that your choices and actions can influence what happens in your life. It includes being able to choose a direction, act on it, and experience yourself as an active participant in shaping what happens next.

Why can too many opportunities make me feel scattered?

When possibilities multiply faster than your ability to evaluate and pursue them, attention can become fragmented. The problem is no longer finding something worth doing; it becomes maintaining direction while new possibilities keep appearing.

Why do I keep changing ideas and starting over?

A new idea can temporarily feel more exciting, urgent, or promising than the work already underway. When that pattern repeats, you may accumulate possibilities faster than evidence that you can stay with one direction long enough to see what it becomes.

What does information overload have to do with agency?

Information expands what you can see, but more information does not automatically create better direction. When the volume of inputs becomes difficult to evaluate, deciding what deserves your attention can become harder.

How does visible progress relate to agency?

Visible progress gives you evidence that your actions are producing movement. That evidence may strengthen self-efficacy — the belief that you can act effectively — which is an important part of personal agency.

Can seeing my own progress help me stop comparing myself with other people?

It can give you a stronger internal reference point. Instead of measuring your life primarily against other people's visible outcomes, you can compare your present actions and progress with your own previous evidence.

What does MyDopa™ have to do with MyAgency?

MyAgency names the larger human challenge. MyDopa™ is designed to make personal progress visible by preserving meaningful evidence from your own life and helping you see what your actions are adding up to over time.

Is MyAgency an ADHD treatment?

No. MyAgency is an educational framework about attention, direction, progress, and personal agency. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care for ADHD or any other medical condition.

Why is MyAgency especially relevant now?

AI, social media, creator culture, constant connectivity, and rapidly expanding opportunity expose people to more possible futures than ever before. MyAgency focuses on the increasingly important ability to encounter those possibilities without surrendering your own direction to them.

Research and Intellectual Context

Where does this framework come from?

MyAgency sits at the intersection of research and ideas about personal agency, self-efficacy, information overload, choice, progress monitoring, attention, and personal development.

Important intellectual context includes Martin Seligman's work on agency, research on self-efficacy and progress monitoring, research on information and choice overload, and real-world observations from people describing the psychological effects of living in environments of rapidly expanding possibility.

MyDopa™ will continue expanding this page as the evidence base develops.

What is the simplest way to describe the idea?

A generation surrounded by possibility does not need another source of possibility. It needs stronger ways to choose, act, stay, see progress, and trust itself.

That is the territory we call MyAgency™.
