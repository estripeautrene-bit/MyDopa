## PAIN-POINT VALIDATION RULE — PERMANENT
*(Added July 16, 2026, after the "Second Wind" research session)*

**MyDopa is a business, not a support resource.** Every content decision is judged by one question first: does this connect us to the largest validated pool of people in real pain we can help — not whether the framing is clever, precise, or original.

**Every article is a source file, not a single output.** One validated pain point becomes a blog article, a long-form video script, a Pinterest pin, a short-form video hook, and a social post — 5+ pieces of content from one research decision. This means the choice of which pain point to write about is multiplied across every format it turns into. A wrong or small choice wastes that multiplication five times over. A right choice pays off five times over. Getting this decision right matters more than it used to, because it's no longer a single article's worth of consequence.

**Before any article gets written, the pain point must be validated at scale, using plain language, not internal cluster names.**
- Internal cluster labels (e.g. "Second Wind / Running on Fumes," "Catch-Up Trap") exist only for our own filing. They are never search terms, never titles, never checked against Reddit or Google as-is.
- Validation means searching broad, ordinary, emotionally specific words a real person would actually type or say — "struggling," "feel like a failure," "not as fun as I used to be" — not a clever or precise phrase invented internally.
- Broad but generic words (e.g. "pain") are too broad and pull in unrelated results (e.g. physical pain instead of emotional pain) — the search word needs to be broad AND emotionally specific.
- A cluster is validated when a plain-language search returns real threads with meaningful engagement (hundreds to thousands of votes/comments), not a single low-engagement example.
- If a cluster's own internal name doesn't return real, on-topic, high-engagement results in a plain search, the cluster gets renamed to match real language before any article is written against it — the mission doesn't change, the language does.

**Example from this session:** "Second Wind / Running on Fumes" returned near-zero relevant results (mostly video games and a media company). A broad plain-language search ("struggling") surfaced a 6,500-vote r/Millennials thread — the single strongest validated thread in the entire vault. The cluster was renamed to match the real language, not the internal label.
