MyDopa™ for Professionals

The session ends. Life continues.

MyDopa helps people preserve what happens in between.

A meaningful conversation can create insight. But the days that follow are where life actually happens.

MyDopa helps people capture lived evidence, reflect on what it may mean, and see progress accumulate over time—so they do not have to rely only on memory when they return to therapy, coaching, or another important conversation.

What happens between conversations matters.

A therapy session ends.

A coaching conversation ends.

A course ends.

A meaningful realization passes.

Then life continues.

People make decisions, handle difficult moments, follow through, avoid things, recover, learn, connect, and change.

Much of that evidence disappears before the next conversation.

MyDopa™ is being built to help preserve it.

A clearer record of what actually happened.

MyToday™
Capture meaningful evidence while life is happening.
A better reaction. A promise kept. A difficult conversation handled differently. A moment of courage, connection, learning, recovery, or follow-through.
MyYesterday™
Reflect on the evidence with context.
MyYesterday helps the user look at what happened, notice what may matter, and preserve continuity without forcing every experience into a positive interpretation.
MyProgress™
See what individual moments become over time.
Patterns, returns, recurring themes, changing responses, and visible evidence of progress begin to accumulate.

Better continuity. Better conversations.

The value to the professional is simple.

Instead of asking a person to reconstruct the last week or two entirely from memory, MyDopa™ may help them return with a richer record of what actually happened.

That record belongs to the user.

They choose what they capture, what they keep, and what they bring into a professional conversation.

MyDopa™ is not designed to replace the therapist, psychologist, coach, or other professional.

It is designed to help the person preserve more of the life that happens between those conversations.

Built with scientific discipline.

MyDopa™ is informed by established research in areas including progress monitoring, self-efficacy, reflection, behavior change, self-regulation, implementation intentions, and longitudinal self-observation.

We are also developing a formal research initiative to study MyDopa™ directly.

The objective is not to borrow scientific authority.

It is to measure carefully, make only claims the evidence supports, and improve the product through serious research.

Questions professionals may have.

What is MyDopa?

MyDopa is a personal-development tool designed to help people capture lived evidence, reflect on what happened, and make progress visible over time.

Is MyDopa therapy?

No. MyDopa is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis care. It is a personal-development and continuity tool designed to support the individual's own practice between meaningful conversations and experiences.

Does MyDopa replace a therapist or coach?

No. MyDopa is designed to complement, not replace, professional relationships. The user decides what, if anything, they bring from MyDopa into therapy, coaching, or another professional conversation.

Can I recommend MyDopa to a client?

Once MyDopa becomes available, professionals may choose to recommend it when they believe the practice could be useful to an individual. We are not building the professional recommendation model around referral commissions.

What does MyDopa share with the professional?

MyDopa belongs to the user. The user chooses what information, if any, they share with a therapist, coach, psychologist, or other professional.

Can professionals help shape MyDopa?

Yes. We are inviting interested professionals to join the Professional Preview so we can learn from the people who work closely with individuals over time.
