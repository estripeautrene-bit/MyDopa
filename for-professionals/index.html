MyDopa™ for Professionals

The session ends. Life continues.

MyDopa™ helps people preserve what happens in between.

A therapy session, coaching conversation, or workshop creates insight. The days that follow show how that insight becomes part of real life.

MyDopa™ helps people capture that part — the everyday decisions, actions, reactions, and moments of progress that happen between meaningful conversations — and turns it into a record they can bring back into the room.

Athletes bring training data to coaches. Patients bring health data to doctors. MyDopa™ gives personal development its own record of what happens between conversations.

How it works

MyToday™

The entry screen. The user records meaningful moments as they happen — a difficult conversation handled well, a boundary held, an intention carried out, or a decision that reflects change.

MyYesterday™

The reflection conversation with Dopa. It helps the user make sense of what they captured in the context of earlier experiences and carry useful context forward.

MyProgress™

The accumulated record over time. It shows recurring themes, intentions and follow-through, periods of consistency, interruptions and returns, and patterns of change that become visible across weeks and months.

Resilience Horizon™ is a view inside MyProgress that shows patterns of engagement over time — periods of consistency, interruption, return, and continuation. It helps the user see how their engagement changes across different periods of life and gives context to what happens after a gap.

The feature surfaces the pattern. The user and, when relevant, the professional can interpret that pattern in context.

MyDopa™ gives personal development something most people have never had: a record of what actually happened between conversations.

Why this matters for your practice

A client arriving with three months of captured intentions, actions, experiences, and outcomes creates a richer starting point for conversation.

When the client chooses to share it, MyDopa™ gives the professional a longitudinal record of what the person actually experienced and applied between conversations.

That record can include recurring themes, follow-through on intentions, changes in response to familiar situations, patterns of engagement and return, and progress that becomes visible through accumulation.

The professional brings expertise, judgment, and context. MyDopa™ gives the person a richer record to bring into the conversation.

The user controls what they capture and what they share.

Questions professionals may have

What is MyDopa?

MyDopa™ is a personal-development app that helps people record meaningful moments, reflect on them, and see their progress accumulate over time.

What is MyToday?

MyToday is the entry screen where the user records meaningful moments from everyday life as they happen.

What is MyYesterday?

MyYesterday is the reflection conversation with Dopa. It works with the user's captured experiences and relevant history to help them understand what those experiences may show.

What is MyProgress?

MyProgress is where the user sees their personal-development record take shape over time.

What is Resilience Horizon?

Resilience Horizon is a MyProgress view that shows the user's pattern of engagement over time, including periods of consistency, interruption, return, and continuation.

How can MyDopa support therapy or coaching?

Clients can bring concrete material from the time between conversations — intentions, actions, recurring themes, changing responses, patterns of engagement, and accumulated progress.

Who owns the information?

The user. They choose what to capture and what to share.

Can I recommend MyDopa to clients?

Yes. Professionals can recommend MyDopa™ when they believe a persistent personal-development record would be useful to the person they work with.
